Trace is a dive, not a ledger

invariant · click a point → it opens into the graph that made it, in its own space → and that into the next, down to the raw bars

No hashes, no DAG to read. A number is a point; inspect it and you fall inside it — into the graph that produced it, drawn in its own space. Keep going and you descend through the analysis, space by space, until you land on the raw bars. The lineage is still exact underneath; you just experience it instead of auditing it.
